FAQ

Got Questions? We've Got Answers

Everything you need to know about Cartron auto parts and services

General Questions

What types of auto parts does Cartron offer?

Cartron offers a wide range of auto parts including engine components, brake systems, lighting solutions, electrical parts, suspension systems, and various car accessories. We stock both OEM and high-quality aftermarket parts for all major vehicle brands.

Are your products genuine and authentic?

Yes, we source all our products directly from authorized manufacturers and distributors. Every part comes with a warranty and is guaranteed to be authentic, ensuring the highest quality and reliability for your vehicle.

Do you offer installation services?

Yes, Cartron provides professional installation services at our Detroit location. Our certified technicians are experts in installing all types of auto parts and can ensure your new components are fitted correctly and safely.

How can I check if a part fits my vehicle?

You can use our vehicle compatibility tool on each product page. Simply enter your vehicle's make, model, year, and engine size to verify fitment. You can also contact our customer support team for assistance in finding the right part.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy for unused items in their original packaging. If you receive a defective or incorrect part, we'll replace it or issue a full refund. Please contact our customer service team to initiate a return.

Do you offer international shipping?

Currently, we ship within the United States and Canada. For international orders, please contact our customer support team and we'll do our best to accommodate your shipping needs.

How can I contact Cartron customer support?

You can reach our customer support team via phone at +1 (800) 555-CARS, email at info@cartron.com, or through our contact form on the website. We're available Monday to Saturday from 8AM to 8PM EST.

Orders & Shipping

01

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping typically takes 3-5 business days within the US. Express shipping is available for 1-2 business day delivery. You'll receive a tracking number via email once your order ships.

02

What are the shipping costs?

We offer free standard shipping on all orders over $50. For orders under $50, shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on your location and chosen shipping method.

03

How can I track my order?

Once your order is shipped, we'll send you a confirmation email with a tracking number. You can use this number on our website or the carrier's website to track your package in real-time.

04

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. All transactions are secured with industry-standard encryption for your safety.

05

Can I cancel or modify my order?

You can cancel or modify your order within 1 hour of placing it. After that, the order may already be processed for shipping. Please contact our support team immediately for any changes.

06

Do you offer bulk discounts for businesses?

Yes, we offer special pricing for bulk orders and commercial accounts. Please contact our business development team at business@cartron.com for a customized quote based on your needs.

07

What if I receive a damaged product?

If you receive a damaged or defective product, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery. We'll arrange for a replacement or full refund and cover the return shipping costs. Your satisfaction is our priority.
